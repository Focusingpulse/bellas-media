## The Core Concept

One messaging core feeds all channels. Not separate tracks — one pitch, expressed differently depending on where it lands.

**The messaging core = the three-level pitch.**
Three problems your audience faces → how you solve them. That IS the elevator pitch. It's also the bio, the postcard hook, the influencer story angle. Same root, different expressions.

**Two pushes, each with its own three-level pitch:**
1. **Bellas House Cleaning Services** (DBA) — local, Gilpin County, print-led
2. **Rental Ready Prep** — Boulder County, digital-led

---

## Push 1: Bellas House Cleaning Services (Gilpin County Local)

### The Three-Level Pitch

**Three problems Gilpin County homeowners face:**

1. **Mountain homes need specialized care.** Altitude dust, well water staining, mud season grime, wildfire ash. A standard cleaner from the city doesn't know how to handle mountain conditions.

2. **You need someone you trust in your home.** Mountain communities are tight. You want a local who knows your area, not a rotating crew of strangers.

3. **Your weekends are for the mountains, not for cleaning.** You live here for the lifestyle. Cleaning is the last thing you want to spend your time on.

**How I solve it:**

I'm Sandra Rose. I've owned Bellas House Cleaning Services since 2016. I live in Gilpin County. I know mountain homes because I clean them every week. Every clean is done by me, not a rotating crew. I handle the altitude dust, the well water, the mud season. You get your weekends back.

Call (970) 212-8485 or visit bellasmountainvacationcleaning.com

### Channel Expressions

**Print (postcards, door hangers, flyers):**
- Front: One problem as a hook question ("Spending your weekend cleaning your mountain home?")
- Back: Three problems condensed to one line each + solution in Sandra's voice + URL + phone
- DBA name "Bellas House Cleaning Services" on the card
- URL prominent: bellasmountainvacationcleaning.com
- Distribution: mail drop in Gilpin County residential areas, door hangers in target neighborhoods, community bulletin boards (Nederland, Black Hawk, Central City)

**PR (bio, introduction, in-person):**
- Sandra's bio IS the three-level pitch expanded into a narrative
- Written bio for press: "Sandra Rose owns Bellas House Cleaning Services, serving Gilpin County since 2016. She started the business after seeing three problems mountain homeowners face: standard cleaners don't know mountain conditions, homeowners can't find a local they trust, and weekends get lost to cleaning. Her solution: owner-operated, mountain-specialized cleaning by someone who lives in the community."
- In-person elevator pitch: the three-level pitch in 30 seconds (three problems, then the solution)
- Target publications: Gilpin County local papers, community blogs, Chamber of Commerce

**Influencer Incubation:**
- The three-level pitch as the story angle for local micro-influencers
- Identify: Gilpin County lifestyle accounts, local community Facebook groups, Nederland/Black Hawk community voices
- Approach: offer value first (a free or discounted clean in exchange for an honest post), build the relationship, let the pitch spread organically through their audience
- Sandra as influencer: her YouTube channel (@Bellas_Mountain_Vacation_Clean) features content around the three problems (mountain cleaning challenges, before/after, local expertise)

## Push 2: Rental Ready Prep (Boulder County)

### The Three-Level Pitch

**Three problems Boulder County property owners face when preparing to list on Airbnb/VRBO:**

1. **Your property isn't guest-ready.** It's lived-in. It needs a deep clean, decluttering, staging. First reviews make or break your listing.

2. **You don't know what STR guests expect.** What to stock in the kitchen? How to stage the living room? What linens to buy? The prep checklist is overwhelming when you're starting from scratch.

3. **Every day unlisted is lost income.** Every week of delay is a week of lost rental revenue. You need to get listed fast, but you can't rush the prep without risking bad first reviews.

**How I solve it:**

Rental Ready Prep by Bellas Mountain Vacation Cleaning. I handle the entire physical prep. Declutter. Deep clean. Stage with what you already own. Restock. Organize. Your property goes from lived-in to listing-ready. You focus on the platform. I handle the space.

Call (970) 212-8485 or visit bellasmountainvacationcleaning.com/rental-ready-prep

### Channel Expressions

**Digital (landing page, targeted outreach):**
- The /rental-ready-prep/ page IS the three-level pitch expanded
- Hero: problem 3 (lost income) as the hook — "Every day your property isn't listed is lost income. Let's get it ready."
- Body: three problems as section headers, solution as the service description
- Targeted outreach: Boulder County STR Facebook groups, Nextdoor, local investor forums
- No print component initially (digital-first for this audience)

**PR (bio, introduction, in-person):**
- Same bio as Push 1, with Rental Ready Prep as the service specialization
- Story angle for Boulder County press: "The mountain STR prep specialist — local cleaner helps Boulder County property owners get listing-ready"
- In-person: the three-level pitch adapted for STR-focused audiences (Boulder County real estate meetups, STR host groups)

**Influencer Incubation:**
- The three-level pitch as the story angle for Boulder County STR community
- Identify: Boulder County STR host accounts, local real estate influencers, mountain lifestyle bloggers
- Approach: offer a Rental Ready Prep demo (before/after content) in exchange for authentic coverage
- Sandra as influencer: YouTube content around STR prep (what guests expect, common prep mistakes, before/after transformations)
