What is a sound bath

It is not complicated. You lie down, someone plays instruments around you, and you listen.

The plain version

A sound bath is an hour of lying down, fully clothed, while sound is played around you. The instruments are mainly singing bowls, plus chimes and a few others. You are not doing anything. You are not trying to relax. You just listen, and your body does the rest on its own.

People describe it differently. Some say their thoughts slow down. Some feel pressure lift off their chest. Some feel nothing much in the moment and sleep better that night. There is no right way for it to go.

Why sound and not silence

Silence in a quiet room is hard to settle into, because your attention goes looking for something. Steady sound gives attention somewhere to rest. That is most of what is happening. The low tones are the part people feel in the body rather than hear with the ears.

What it is not

It is not a medical treatment and I do not present it as one. I am not a therapist or a doctor, and a sound bath does not diagnose, cure or treat anything.

If you are pregnant, have a seizure disorder, have a pacemaker or another implanted device, or are working through recent trauma, talk to your doctor first and tell me before you book. I would rather change the session than have you sit through something that is wrong for you.

Who tends to like it

  • People who cannot switch off, and have tried everything else
  • Couples who want an hour that is not a dinner or a movie
  • Anyone who has been carrying a hard stretch for a while
  • People who are curious and have never done anything like this

What people most often ask

Whether they will be able to relax. Usually yes, and usually by about ten minutes in. Whether they have to believe in anything. No. Whether it is awkward. Only for the first few minutes, and then it is not.
