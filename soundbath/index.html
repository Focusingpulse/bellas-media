Stillness you can feel

A sound bath is an hour where nothing is asked of you. You lie down, you close your eyes, and the sound does the rest.

Quiet the noise

A nervous system settles when it has something steady to follow. Sound gives it something steady to follow, and you do not have to try.

Whole body

Low frequencies are felt more than heard. Most people notice it in their chest before they notice it anywhere else.

Room to reset

For one hour nobody needs anything from you. No phone, no talking, no decisions. That part alone is worth the drive.

The room

Private sound bath sessions

Sessions are held in my home in the mountains west of Denver. It is a quiet space, set up for one person or two. No cold studio, no group, no strangers.

You feel the sound in your body. There are no headphones. The room runs on a full range system, so the low end you feel in your chest is real sound in the room, not a recording pushed into your ears.

The experience

An hour that is only yours

You arrive, you take your shoes off, and you lie down. I play the bowls and a few other instruments around you, slowly, for about an hour. There is nothing to follow and nothing to learn.

Some people see color. Some people cry a little and feel better after. Most people just go somewhere quiet in their head and come back rested.

Questions people ask

If yours is not here, ask me when you book.

What do I wear?

Anything you can lie down in. Loose is better. Shoes come off at the door.

Do I need experience?

No. Most people who come have never done one. You lie down and you listen. That is the whole thing.

How long is it?

About an hour of sound, plus a few minutes on either side to arrive and to come back.

What if I fall asleep?

People do, often. It still works. I will wake you gently at the end.

Ready when you are

Sessions are by appointment. Tell me a day that suits you and I will send you what to expect.
