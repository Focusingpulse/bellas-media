What happens next

I read every request myself and reply within a day. You will get the address, directions and a short note on how to prepare.

If the day you want is already taken, I will offer you the closest one I have.

Before you book

If you are pregnant, have a seizure disorder, have a pacemaker or another implanted device, or are working through recent trauma, please talk to your doctor first and tell me in the notes. I would rather adjust the session than have it be wrong for you.
