The experience

An hour in a quiet room

Private sessions in my home, for one person or two. Here is exactly what happens.

The room

You arrive

Shoes off at the door. There is a short conversation first, so I know how your week has been and whether you want something gentle or something deeper. Then you lie down on a padded mat with a blanket, and you get as comfortable as you can.

The hour

I play the bowls around you, moving slowly from one side of the room to the other. It starts soft and gets fuller. Most people stop tracking time somewhere in the first ten minutes.

There are no headphones and nothing to watch. The sound is in the room with you. The low tones are the ones you feel more than hear, and that is on purpose.

If you fall asleep, that is fine and it happens often. I will wake you gently at the end rather than letting you come out of it suddenly.

The practical part

Where
My home in the mountains west of Denver, Colorado. The address and directions come with your booking confirmation.
Who it is for
One person, or two together. Sessions are private. There is never a group and never anyone else in the room.
How long
About an hour of sound, plus a few minutes at each end. Plan on ninety minutes door to door.
What to wear
Anything you can lie down in comfortably. Loose is better than fitted.
What to bring
Nothing. Mats, pillows and blankets are here. Bring your own pillow if you would rather.
After
Give yourself a slow half hour if you can. People who rush straight back to traffic tend to lose most of it.
Before you book
Tell me about pregnancy, a seizure disorder, an implanted device, or recent trauma. I will adapt the session or tell you honestly if it is not right for you.

Book your hour

Tell me a day that suits you and I will send you the address and what to expect.
